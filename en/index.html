Arrival and departure
Check-in3:00 pm (15:00)
Check-out12:00 pm (noon)

Once inside the condominium, you enter with the temporary code for the digital lock that you receive when your booking is confirmed. The apartment is on the ground floor, with no stairs or elevator.

Please bring towels and bed sheets with pillowcases. They are not included, and neither are pool floaties.

Beds

  • Main bedroom: 1 double bed
  • Second and third bedrooms: 2 single beds each (twin or twin-plus sheets fit)

When you arrive

  • Turn on the water heater. It takes 2 to 3 hours to reach a good temperature for use.
  • Terrace key. It is a small key for the terrace sliding windows, and it is on the console table in the entry hall of the apartment. The windows are normally closed but not locked. If the latch is sunken in, you will need to use it. There is a tutorial on the correct use of the terrace on Instagram @almapuyai.

Before you leave

  • Pull the handle up. Every time you go out and the apartment is left empty, pull the handle of the smart lock upward. This engages the second lock and adds security to the condominium.
  • Leave the refrigerator plugged in. At check-out, leave it free of food and ice, and plugged in.
  • Leave the trash in well-tied bags in the shaft, as explained in the Pool, grill area and trash section.

  • Second bathroom fan: it is automatic and runs from time to time. It is not connected to the bathroom light switch.
  • Door chain: it is for security and to keep small children from leaving the apartment on their own.
  • Beds: please do not push them against the wall, to avoid damaging the wallpaper in the bedrooms.
Take care of the plumbing. Do not flush toilet paper rolls, wet wipes, diapers or anything that could clog the pipes. The condominium has already had overflows because of this.
House rules

In the apartment

  • Maximum capacity: 6 people.
  • Background noise only. High-power sound systems are not allowed.
  • Smoking only in the grill area and on the terrace, with the windows closed.

In the condominium

  • Speed limit of 15 km/h (9 mph). Children play here.
  • Use only your apartment's parking space. If in doubt, ask at the front desk.
  • Park with the front of the car facing the inner street, so you can leave quickly in an emergency. The fine is 0.5 UF.
  • Children must not play on stairs, in common areas or at the vehicle entrance.
  • Do not hang clothes or towels on the terraces. Use foldable drying racks.
  • Alcohol only in the grill area. It is not allowed in gardens, pools, parking areas or hallways.
  • Pets must be on a leash, their waste must be picked up, and they may not enter the pool area.
  • Traffic cones mark safe-speed zones. If one makes it hard to park, you may move it and put it back in place.

Trash and recycling

The shaft is at the entrance of the building. Throw trash in well-tied bags, leave glass and plastic bottles on the shelves, and put cardboard inside the shaft. If the bag does not fit, leave it in the collection rooms. Do not leave it at the tower entrance.
